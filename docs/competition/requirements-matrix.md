## Priority reconciliation

The pasted task's P0/P1/P2 labels govern the implementation order. The rendered brief's strikethroughs and footnotes lower Accelevents, resources/wiki, and embeddable public views to optional/bonus from the competition's perspective; they remain in the matrix because the supplied task explicitly requests them after P0. AI review remains optional and must never displace human evaluation.
