# Personas

## Organizer / admin

Owns the event and needs a fast answer to “what will stop this session from going live?” They configure forms, review decisions, tasks, messages, schedule placements, and integrations. They need explicit save state, audit history, conflict explanations, and safe bulk actions.

## Speaker / submitter

May be nontechnical and usually completes work on a phone between meetings. They need event context, one clear next action, file upload feedback, due dates, and a secure link that does not expose another speaker’s data.

## Evaluator

Reviews assigned submissions quickly and consistently. They need a visible rubric, progress, blind-review behavior, conflict abstention, saved progress, and no access to unassigned records.

## Public attendee

Wants to find a speaker or session on a phone. They need fast search, explicit timezone, track/room filters, deep links, and an itinerary that does not require an organizer account.

## Integration operator

Owns one-way synchronization and may be troubleshooting bad credentials. They need validation, dry-run diffs, idempotent retries, per-record status, last-success state, and logs that never reveal secrets.
